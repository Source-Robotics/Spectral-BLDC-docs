# Mounting and wiring

How the driver goes onto the motor and what gets connected to it: the encoder magnet on the shaft, the board on a
bracket behind the motor, the three phases to the motor terminals, the supply and the CAN bus, and the thermistor.
Do the pages in order; the magnet first, because it is the one thing you cannot check from the outside later.
